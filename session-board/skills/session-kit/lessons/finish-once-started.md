---
name: finish-once-started
description: "Once a task is started, carry it through to the end; don't stop midway to ask \"should I carry on?\""
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

When the person asks for something ("start", "set it up"), keep going until it is done and verified. Give a short progress line if needed, but don't end the turn to ask permission to continue.

**Why:** stopping halfway with "Should I carry on?" costs the person a round trip for a question whose answer is almost always yes.

**How to apply:** stop only for a real blocker or a decision that is genuinely theirs. Otherwise pick a sensible default, say which one in a single line, and continue.
